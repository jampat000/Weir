# Weir v3.2.8

Date: 2026-09-27

This release remembers your light or dark choice with your account, so Weir looks the same wherever you sign in.

## Fixes

- **Your theme follows you.** Weir used to remember light or dark mode only in the browser where you chose it. Another browser, another device or another address for the same Weir showed the system's own setting instead; for example, the tray opens `localhost` while another PC uses the server's IP address. The choice is now saved with your account and applied wherever you sign in. Other Weir tabs you have open switch straight away, and each browser keeps a local copy so pages still open in your theme without a flash.
- Until you pick a theme, Weir still follows your system's light or dark setting. The sign-in page does the same, because it cannot know who you are yet.

## Upgrade Notes

- **This release changes the database.** A migration adds each account's theme choice, empty until the account chooses one. A 3.2.8 database cannot be opened by 3.2.7 or earlier.
- **You keep your current choice.** The first time you sign in after upgrading, the theme this browser already remembers is saved to your account and from then on follows you everywhere.
- Windows: **Check for updates** in the tray or **System › About**, or install `Weir-win-Setup.exe` over the top.
- Docker: `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`.
